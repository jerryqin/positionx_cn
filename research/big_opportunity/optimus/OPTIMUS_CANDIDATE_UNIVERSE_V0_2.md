# Optimus Candidate Universe V0.2 — 2026-10-08

Purpose: replace the 7-name seed basket with a broader, evidence-admitted A-share research universe for the prospective Optimus case.

This is **not** a Tesla supplier list and **not** a ranking of investment attractiveness.

## Admission contract

A company may enter when all are true:
1. It maps to a core humanoid/Optimus BOM or production-chain domain.
2. There is PIT-auditable company/report/IR evidence of a relevant product, customer validation, sample, order, shipment, revenue, or dedicated capacity.
3. Economic relevance is plausible enough to matter if volume ramps.
4. Tesla/Optimus linkage is recorded separately and never inferred from generic robotics exposure.

Exclusions / fail-closed rules:
- media-only thematic association;
- broker-only “beneficiary” labels without company/product evidence;
- stock-price strength as supplier evidence;
- policy support without commercialization evidence;
- post-hoc promotion after later price action.

## Status semantics
- `CONFIRMED`: explicit public Tesla/Optimus supplier/order/shipment evidence.
- `PROBABLE`: strong robotics commercialization plus strong but unnamed/indirect customer linkage; still **not** supplier confirmation.
- `POSSIBLE`: verified core-product commercialization, but Tesla/Optimus customer identity is unconfirmed.
- `SPECULATIVE`: strategically relevant product/technology, but commercialization or customer evidence is still early/weak.
- `REMOVED`: contradicted, immaterial, or no longer relevant.

## Important boundary
The V0.1 seven-company M-Evidence snapshot remains valid as a **Seed Basket snapshot**, but it must not be interpreted as the whole Optimus China supply-chain breadth. V0.2 expands the universe and recalculates M-Evidence on the admitted set.
